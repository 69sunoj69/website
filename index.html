03Process

How I approach the work.

The work is bigger than the drawing. Every build begins with listening and is finished with care.

  1. Step 01

    Listen & understand

    Start with the site, the brief and the people involved, so the requirements are clear before anything is drawn.

  2. Step 02

    Plan the work

    Break the project into clear priorities, sequences and responsibilities that the whole team can follow.

  3. Step 03

    Draw it precisely

    Turn decisions into accurate drawings that leave little room for guesswork on site.

  4. Step 04

    Build responsibly

    Follow through with safety, quality and open communication from start to handover.

—Working principle

Plan carefully. Build responsibly.
